ERA5 is the fifth-generation global atmospheric reanalysis produced by ECMWF, the European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts, as part of the Copernicus Climate Change Service. A reanalysis combines historical observations with a weather model to produce a complete, consistent record of past weather.

ERA5 provides hourly data from 1940 to the present on a grid of about 0.25 by 0.25 degrees, roughly 31 km. It includes wind components at 10 m and 100 m above ground (u10, v10, u100, v100), temperature, pressure and solar radiation. Data can be downloaded free from the Copernicus Climate Data Store using the cdsapi Python package.

In wind resource assessment ERA5 is mainly used as a long-term reference for MCP and for checking inter-annual variability. Because a grid cell is about 31 km wide, ERA5 cannot resolve local terrain effects and often under- or over-estimates absolute wind speeds at a specific site. It should therefore be used to describe long-term trends and variability, not as a replacement for on-site measurements.
